What We Can Do

Reduce & Recycle

Cut down single-use plastics and recycle paper, glass and metal products to minimize waste and pollution.

Plant More Trees

Trees absorb carbon dioxide and produce oxygen. Participate in tree-planting activities to combat climate change.

Save Water

Fix leaking taps, collect rainwater and use water-efficient appliances to conserve this precious resource.

Use Clean Energy

Choose solar, wind or other renewable energy sources to reduce reliance on fossil fuels and lower carbon emissions.
